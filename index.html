Frequently Asked Questions (Tambayoyin Da Aka Saba Yi)

 
How does “Buy & Source for Me” work?
Tell us what you need and where to find it: a market, a store, or anywhere in Kano. You can send photos of what you want on WhatsApp. Once your payment is confirmed, we find it, send you photos and the price, buy when you approve, and deliver. Some shops give receipts and some do not. If there is none, we send a photo of the goods and the shop, plus the amount paid.

 
How do payments work? (Yadda ake biya)
Your order starts once payment is confirmed. We send you the total on WhatsApp: item money, our sourcing fee and the delivery fare. Pay by bank transfer, and we start as soon as we confirm it. If your items cost less than the money you sent, we return the difference. If they cost more, we ask you to top up before we buy. We will never ask for your password or OTP.

 
What are your hours and delivery times?
Monday to Saturday 8am-6pm, and Sunday 12pm-7pm. After closing until 9pm costs +₦1,000 and up. Express starts within about 1 hour and usually takes 2-3 hours. Pooled (grouped runs): order by 11am for about 3pm, order by 2pm for about 6pm, later orders go next morning. On Sundays we start at 12pm, so runs go out later. Planning ahead? Choose a date and time in the form. Hard-to-find items take longer, and we will tell you.

 
Can I just send a package?
Yes. Choose Pick Up & Deliver to send a parcel, food or documents between any two places in Kano, for example from your shop to your customer.

 
Can you carry heavy or full loads?
Yes, we can carry plenty. Tick “full load” in the form. Full loads cost more than normal fares, and we confirm the price on WhatsApp, so a photo of the load helps.

 
What if you cannot find my item?
We tell you on WhatsApp. If we find none of your items, the search fee covers our trip. If we find some, you pay for those, the sourcing fee, and delivery.

 
📄 Policies

 
Refunds and lost goods
If we cannot find or buy your item, the item money you sent is returned in full. A search fee, agreed beforehand, covers our trip. If goods are lost or damaged while in our care, we will refund or replace the item cost shown by the receipt or proof of purchase.

 
Items we do not carry
Illegal goods, weapons, drugs, flammable or hazardous items, live animals, and cash.

 
Privacy
We collect your name, phone number, and addresses only to complete your order. We do not sell your data. As you type a location, the text is sent to a free map-search service (Photon, using OpenStreetMap data) to suggest places. If you tap “Use my current location”, a map link with your position is added to your WhatsApp message. This website does not store it. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.
